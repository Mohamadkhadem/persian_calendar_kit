## 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* `JalaliDate`, `GregorianDate`, `HijriDate`, `JalaliDateRange`.
* `JalaliFormatter`, `PersianDigits`, `PersianRelativeTime`.
* Persian, Dari, Kurdish and English strings.
* Iranian official holidays and occasions; rule based custom events.
* `PersianCalendar`, `PersianMonthView`, year / month pickers,
  `showPersianDatePicker`, `showPersianDateRangePicker`,
  `showPersianMultiDatePicker`, `PersianWheelDatePicker`,
  `PersianDateFormField`.
* Theming via `PersianCalendarThemeData` (ThemeExtension) and builders.
* `CalendarConverter`, Unix / ISO 8601 / Tehran time conversions.
* Pattern parsing for Jalali, Hijri and Gregorian dates; numbers and dates
  in Persian words.
* `JalaliPeriod`, `JalaliCalendar` and `JalaliDateUtils` (age, business days,
  nth weekday, seasons, zodiac, animal year), `PersianDurationFormat`.
* `PersianWeekView`, per-day decoration / style / content builders, header
  layouts, custom navigation icons and vertical paging.
